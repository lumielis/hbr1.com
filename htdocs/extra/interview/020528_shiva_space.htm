We met DJ Joerg from SHIVA SPACE. We spoke with him about his last tour and something more. He made an interview with australien magazin ZEBRA. He gives it to us, so we are proudly to present you some interesting opinions.

ZEBRA: How's India? What have you been doing there?

Joerg: Hello, i had a nice Party in India / Mumbay! very professional club, a lot of people and all and all very nice! I really love India for a long time, as everybody knows!

ZEBRA: You've had a long involvement in the trance music scene - what do you think are the biggest changes you've seen over that time? You've also had a strong involvement in Germany. What kind of differences are there between the European scene and the rest of the world?

Joerg: This musik was allways changing in all this years that i follow that scene of trancemusic! More and better equipment, better production, let this music change all the time! For my point is the scene in Germany - not all for sure - a little boring to me! Mostly all the DJs are tuned in minimal progressive trance, what i really like, i play it by my self for start the party, or long party late afternooon, cool! What i really don’t like is, that i cant hear anymore changes when the DJs are changing and you have partys on 136bpm only! Sorry, but a hole party... same style musik... not for me! I love this musik, becauce it aloud me changes of styles and i can go where ever i want! There is no ultimate style of trance ...all the styles together makes a good party!!

ZEBRA: At the same time, a lot of the US seems to be a lot slower at catching on to more interesting sounds. how do you feel about the fact that a good part of America is unaware of the development of house in their own country?

Joerg: I am unaware about that kind of movement in US, sorry! I played there more times allready, NY, Miami or L.A.! I had good partys there but allways the crowd was one third Japanese, one third Israelis and one third Americans! I still think for a big country like this, trance is still unknown and very underground! It needs more good outdoor partys in the US, that will make it grow faster!

ZEBRA: With your releases, you've really tried to push trance music in new directions without pushing it off the dancefloor. Do you think that people are becoming more receptive more experimental sounds? Why or why not?

Joerg: As i say, i am very aware that all this different styles makes for me a good party! All of that is one story for me! I used to play long partys alone, for many hours and i really wish all DJs that kind of experience! May be just to realize that you need all of that music to hold people for long time! That is what will unite all of that partypeople and artists and lables, becauce all off them are together playt in one party! If it is only one lable one direction....... boring!

ZEBRA: With many of your releases you manage to incorporate elements of trance, progressive, and psychedelic - are you generally aware of all these elements? How do you generally compose a track?

Joerg: Every human have different moods in diffirent times! I believe in the recept of Indian Raga... the right sound in the right moment! Same way i start a track... if Skazi is guest in my house i do maximal with him... and mostly after i feel like making ambient, just for the ballance! What ever you feel, also by DJing! Not the best technical mix, like it is, so camen from the house scene is hear so importent! I believe in story telling, i am a storyteller not a DJ! That makes it less serious and more funny.... and is it not only about having F U N

ZEBRA: How was the Shiva Space technology name set up? You've also worked with heaps of different producers - do you find you have a strong community of producers in Germany - and around the world - ?

Joerg: The name i gave to my Techno bus in India it represented everything that was important to me in that time!!! On a quest for the Dancing Shiva the technology to make the tracks to make him dance and to send him to SPACE out ! hihi Yeah i love to work with different musicans, its fun to learn more things and get better and better! And also there we have mostly a lot of fun and i allways wanted to know the person behind the track! In the moment i live in Japan and opened there Shiva Space Japan.... so i am not so often in Germany lately, my partner Andy is living and playing a lot in Germany and takes care of our lable and the production in Germany! Just released in Shiva Space Technology his own project, with his partner Janosh, Lemurians Album! And there is in nearly every country a strong community of trance people... i like to jump into all of them, that make me feel coming home in every country on this our planet EARTH! I like the idea of a trancing dancing planet with people how having a good time! No wars less stress and a easyer live with lot of partys all over! Is there a good psychedelic scene in germany as i m saying, i am not so often there, but i hope that psychedelic trance will be bit more inclouded again! German has nice artists and producers, very nice events also!

ZEBRA: Do you find there's a strong community between all the producers you work with? Trance seems to have had its ups and downs in terms of popularity over the past decade or so - how do you react to the media and how they tend to create these trends?

Joerg: The live have up and downs, same way trance musik you just have to enjoy it, i guess thats why we are here for! But in the last years i see the movement growing all over that planet... sometimes slowly but continious! We should think big and change things on this planet.. may be the god s will give it to us!

ZEBRA: And... what do you get up to when you're not djing, producing, etc...?

Joerg: Hi hi.. i must say that i live in sound studios, but if i not then i like to seat with my lovely doughter Tenshi or have fun... anyway!
LOVE and LIGHT and keep the Spirit alive....

DJ Joerg
