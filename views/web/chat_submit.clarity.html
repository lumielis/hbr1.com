{# The 29 px submit bar - production `apax.cgi?Part=Chat&Action=submit`. Production put the Nickname and Message fields inside the frame's dark-blue input chrome (`/images/chat/inp*.gif`), with an OK button. Two things about the geometry are load-bearing and easy to undo: * The bar is the *bottom* frame of the chat frameset, so it must sit at the bottom of the viewport. that wraps the rows; standards mode does not propagate through the form at all, so every link in the chain is declared explicitly. * The four chrome gifs are not uniformly aligned. `inpl`/`inpr` are 29 px and fill the bar; `inptl`/`inptr` are 9 px tabs that must hug the top.
